★ Executive summary

The one-platform outcome, without the rip-and-replace risk.

Aquaventure welcomes more than a million guests a year, and every one of them touches admission, access, a credential, and in-park spend — the layer Vantage already runs. We agree with the goal behind this request: one seamless guest experience, and one source of truth. We disagree only on the means. The reliable, lower-risk path to that goal is an operating OS over best-of-breed systems — not a monolith that bolts systems together and calls it one platform. What follows is a plain-spoken map of every requirement: what Vantage owns, what Vantage orchestrates through integration, and what stays with your finance and ticketing systems by design. Where we are not a perfect fit, we say so.

1

Best-of-breed, unified — not a monolith.

A single-vendor platform looks complete on paper, but it is best-in-class at nothing, and it hides fragmentation behind one logo — separate success managers, data that does not talk. Vantage is the operating OS that lets Atlantis run the best ticketing and the best of everything, then unifies the guest and the data across all of it. One seamless guest experience and one source of truth, without a vendor’s compromises.

2

The platform you shaped — now productized.

Vantage began at Aquaventure as a custom build. It is now the productized platform the industry buys. Replacing the custom modules with the platform means Atlantis inherits the roadmap — new capability every quarter, instead of paying to invent each feature.

3

Live today — continuity, not disruption.

The hardware is installed, the integrations are proven, customer success is embedded with your teams, and migration risk is low. Every alternative is a multi-year rip-and-replace with fresh hardware cost. Vantage is already running Aquaventure. Yes — there is new UI and training. The difference is that the integration is proven live, not promised.

No other system knows all of it: ticketing plus in-park behaviour plus dwell plus spend, tied to one guest.
